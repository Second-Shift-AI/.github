## Why the name

<div align="center">
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Second-Shift-AI/.github/main/profile/assets/handover.gif" alt="Two offset bars: the first shift fades out as the second takes over, with the handoff traveling between them." width="720">
</div>

In a plant that runs around the clock, the second shift is the crew that takes
over when the first goes home. The work does not stop, it changes hands.

The name carries an obligation with it. A shift that takes over is expected to
hand back cleanly, with a written record of what happened while everyone else
was away. Most automation projects fail on exactly that point rather than on
the technology.

Four weeks to handover, on fixed scope. It runs on your accounts, your keys and
your existing stack. Documentation, a reach list, measured running cost and an
off switch are included in every build. A model we resell you at a markup is
not.
